Application Process

This segment invites innovative solutions that address social and developmental challenges through technology, community-based approaches, service delivery innovation, inclusive business models, partnerships, or process improvements.

Solution may leverage emerging technologies such as Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), Data Analytics, FinTech Solutions, Mobile Applications, Blockchain, Digital Platforms, Assistive Technologies, IoT where relevant, but technology is not mandatory.

Eligibility Criteria

Open to individuals, startups, social enterprises, academic institutions, NGOs, innovators, and organizations.
Must address one or more focus areas under Inclusive Innovation for Bharat.
Should demonstrate innovation, feasibility, and measurable impact.
Solutions should include a prototype, pilot, proof of concept, or demonstrable model.
Practical, scalable, and sustainable implementation pathways must be outlined.
