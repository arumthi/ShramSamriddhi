## [0:00 - 0:45] The Hook & The Problem (Empathy + Core Gap)
Speaker:
"Judges, meet Ramesh. He is a 32-year-old seasonal agricultural laborer. When the harvest ends, he moves to the city to work on construction sites. He works 12 hours a day, handles entirely physical cash, and belongs to India’s 400-million-strong unorganized workforce.
Corporate employees like us have automated salaries, health insurance, and a 12% matching corporate EPF. Ramesh has none. If he stops working, his income drops to zero. If he gets injured, his family falls into debt.
The tragic part? The government has pension and insurance schemes for him—like PM-SYM and PMSBY. But Ramesh doesn’t know they exist, doesn't know how to navigate the digital portals, and cannot commit to rigid monthly premiums because his daily wage fluctuates violently.
The highway of social security exists, but the last-mile vehicle is completely broken. Until today."

## [0:45 - 1:30] The Solution & Demo (Product + Technical Architecture)
Speaker:
"Introducing ShramSetu, the unified digital bridge that merges hyper-local job matching with an automated, frictionless social security wallet.
When Ramesh opens our app, he sees a clean, voice-first interface in his regional language. Our hyper-local backend matching engine instantly connects him to nearby verified agricultural hubs and contractors. But here is where the magic happens:

* The Invisible EPF: When Ramesh completes a daily gig and receives a ₹500 payout via our app, our transaction layer automatically routes a user-defined micro-pension fraction—say 5%, or ₹25—directly into his long-term retirement pool.
* The Contractor Match: To access our verified, rated labor supply, the hiring contractor contributes a nominal platform premium that co-matches a portion of Ramesh's daily pension, bringing corporate-style benefits to the informal economy.
* AI Welfare Radar: On the technical backend, our platform takes basic e-KYC telemetry and matches Ramesh's profile against national welfare portals. The system proactively prompts him via voice: 'You qualify for the ₹2 Lakh accidental insurance cover for just ₹20/year. Press 1 to activate instantly.' "

## [1:30 - 2:15] Technical Feasibility & The Tech Stack (For Technical Judges)
Speaker:
"We aren't reinventing the wheel; we are combining existing infrastructure via open APIs.
Our system architecture leverages India Stack at its core. We utilize Aadhaar e-KYC and DigiLocker APIs for friction-free, paperless onboarding. For the continuous stream of micro-savings, we utilize UPI 2.0 Auto-Pay mandates and the Aadhaar Enabled Payment System (AePS), ensuring that even workers with basic smartphones or feature phones can participate.
All job histories, ratings, and daily pension allocations are recorded on a secure transactional ledger database. This architectural ledger builds an alternative cryptographic credit score for Ramesh. By proving his steady employment history on ShramSetu, he can bypass predatory local moneylenders and unlock formal micro-loans from partner banks."
